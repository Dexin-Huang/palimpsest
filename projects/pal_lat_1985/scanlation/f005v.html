Significance

This drawing is part of a Trionfi cycle, likely inspired by Petrarch's *Trionfi*. The inclusion of historical and philosophical figures like Cicero, Aristotle, Archimedes, Ptolemy, Pythagoras, and Hippocrates suggests an allegorical representation of the triumph of Sobriety/Diligence over worldly knowledge and achievement. The presence of Mercury, the god of commerce, eloquence, and messages, adds another layer of meaning, possibly linking Sobriety/Diligence to success and prosperity. The inclusion of Orpheus and Memory suggests the importance of art and remembrance in achieving Sobriety/Diligence. The figure of Sobriety holding keys could symbolize the unlocking of virtues or secrets through temperance. The choice of figures and their attributes is carefully considered to convey the overall allegorical message. This particular combination of figures and the specific triumph being depicted are unusual and warrant further investigation.
