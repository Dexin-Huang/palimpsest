Significance

This page is part of a Trionfi cycle, likely inspired by Petrarch's *Trionfi*. The triumph of Libido is a less common subject than the triumphs of Love, Chastity, Death, Fame, Time, and Eternity, making this manuscript potentially unique. The inclusion of Ovid and Tibullus, Roman poets known for their erotic verse, is appropriate for the triumph of Libido. The presence of satyrs and Bacchantes further reinforces the theme of sensual pleasure and indulgence. The architectural background suggests a classical setting, consistent with the overall theme. The manuscript's lack of prior study makes it a valuable resource for understanding the visual culture of the 16th century and the reception of Petrarch's *Trionfi*.
