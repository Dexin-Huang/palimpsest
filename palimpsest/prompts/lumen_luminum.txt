## MANUSCRIPT IDENTIFICATION

**Manuscript:** Pal.lat.1267 (Vatican Library, Bibliotheca Palatina)
**Date:** 14th century
**Script:** Gothic textualis
**Text:** "Lumen Luminum" (Light of Lights)

### About This Text

The **Lumen Luminum** is an alchemical recipe collection originally by **al-Razi** (Rhazes), translated to Latin in the 12th-13th century. In this manuscript it is attributed to **Pseudo-Aristotle**.

**Incipit:** "Hic incipiunt receptus de libro magistri Aristotilis qui dicitur lumen luminum"
(Here begin the recipes from the book of Master Aristotle which is called Light of Lights)

### Text Structure

The Lumen Luminum is organized into sections (rationes) covering:
1. **De spiritibus** - On spirits (volatile substances)
2. **De salibus** - On salts
3. **De aluminibus** - On alums
4. **De atramentis** - On vitriols
5. **De corporibus** - On bodies (metals)
6. **De mineralibus** - On minerals

Each recipe typically begins with "Accipe..." (Take...) or "Recipe..." (Take...)

## THE MINIM PROBLEM

In Gothic script, these letters are identical vertical strokes (minims):
- **i** = 1 minim
- **u, n** = 2 minims each
- **m** = 3 minims

**Strategy:** Count minims, test against Latin vocabulary.

| Looks like | Likely is | Verification |
|------------|-----------|--------------|
| "inuenies" | **invenies** | Common verb "you will find" |
| "unium" | **vinum** | Context: wine/liquid? |

## RUBRICS AND STRUCTURE

**Rubrics** (red text) mark sections:
- ¶ = paragraph/section marker
- "Capitulum de..." = Chapter on...
- "De [substance]" = On [substance]
- "Separatio..." = Separation of...
- "Purificatio..." = Purification of...

**Decorated initials:**
- Large ornate letters (2-8 lines tall) begin major sections
- Often blue with red flourishes, or red with blue
- "H" for "Hic incipiunt..."
- "A" for "Accipe..."
- "R" for "Recipe..."

## GOTHIC LETTER CONFUSIONS

| Confused | How to distinguish |
|----------|-------------------|
| c / t | t has crossbar right |
| c / e | e has tongue inside |
| f / ſ (long s) | f has crossbar both sides |
| u / n | IDENTICAL - use context |
| a / o / u | Round - check context |

### Long S (ſ) Rules
- Word-initial: ſ (long s)
- Word-medial: ſ (long s)
- Word-final: s (round s)
