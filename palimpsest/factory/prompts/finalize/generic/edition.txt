# Final Edition Editor

## Goal

Produce the final reader-facing prose for a source-grounded bilingual edition.
The evidence layers are finished. Your job is to make the book internally
consistent: every English translation must translate the final emended reading,
and the heading and reader's note must accurately describe what the evidence
supports.

You are not another paleographer and you are not a publisher. Do not alter the
diplomatic original, emended reading, apparatus, section boundaries, or page
membership. The deterministic publisher will combine your prose with those
immutable layers after this task.

## Evidence

- `evidence/manuscript.json`: ordered sections, page membership, diplomatic
  originals, draft translations, joins, and the draft reader's note.
- `evidence/emendations.json`: the final reading for each section and the
  apparatus explaining every departure from the diplomatic original.
- `evidence/reference.json`: bounded identification and external evidence.
- `evidence/transcription_audits.json`: page-level reading routes, candidate
  readings, adjudication reasoning, and unresolved visual or segmentation issues.

Treat the final emended reading as the authoritative text, but not as permission
to erase surviving source uncertainty. Use the transcription audits to distinguish
what the image readers and adjudicator actually established from what the reference
dossier proposes. The apparatus may resolve a cited source span; otherwise retain
an audit's unresolved graph, segmentation, damage, or alternative explicitly in
English. Treat the reference dossier as testimony, not permission to silently
improve the source. Never invent a name, quantity, date, connective, or continuous
passage to make damaged writing read smoothly.

## Required passes

### 1. Orient

Read all four evidence files before drafting. Establish the document's identity,
genre, physical structure, and limits. Check the transcription audits before
accepting a fluent draft reading. Check whether the draft reader's note or headings
overstate coherence, certainty, date, authorship, or identification.

### 2. Edit every section in order

For section index `0`, then `1`, and so on:

1. Compare its diplomatic original and draft translation in `manuscript.json`
   with its final `reading` in `emendations.json`.
2. Read every apparatus entry belonging to that section. Propagate every
   meaning-bearing correction into English, especially personal and institutional
   names, quantities, dates, commodities, negation, and the removal of false
   continuous text.
4. Check the relevant page in `transcription_audits.json`. If an unresolved
   visual or segmentation issue affects the final reading and is not explicitly
   resolved by the apparatus, preserve that uncertainty in English.
5. Translate the final reading, not the machine draft and not the reference
   dossier. Use the draft only when it remains supported.
6. Preserve paragraph and list structure where the source supports it. Prefer
   direct, readable English over commentary embedded in the translation.
7. Preserve uncertainty explicitly and locally. Use `[?]`, alternatives, or a
   concise bracketed description where needed; do not turn an unresolved graph,
   damaged quantity, or uncertain segmentation into fluent prose.
8. Give the section a concise descriptive heading justified by its actual
   contents. A set of isolated marks is not a fragmentary continuous work.

Do not omit a section, merge sections, split sections, or reorder them.

### 3. Reconcile the whole edition

Write a reader's note that accurately introduces the physical object, contents,
probable context, and major limitations. Keep bounded identification distinct
from certainty. Make terminology, units, names, tense, and uncertainty notation
consistent across sections. Check every claim against the final readings and the
reference dossier.

### 4. Verify

Before writing the artifact, verify:

- there is exactly one output entry for every input section;
- `section_index` values are consecutive integers beginning with `0`;
- no English passage contradicts an apparatus correction;
- deleted or rejected source text does not survive as translated prose;
- every unresolved source span remains visibly unresolved;
- no transcription-audit uncertainty silently becomes a definite word or event;
- headings and the reader's note do not claim more coherence than the source;
- no evidence layer was modified.

## Output contract

Write exactly one JSON object to `out/edition.json`:

```json
{
  "readers_note": "Final reader-facing introduction.",
  "sections": [
    {
      "section_index": 0,
      "heading": "Final descriptive heading",
      "translation": "English translation of the final emended reading."
    }
  ]
}
```

No additional top-level keys are needed. Every string must be non-empty. Write
valid UTF-8 JSON without Markdown fences.
