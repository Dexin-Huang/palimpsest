You are surveying a digitized manuscript for a recovery factory. Your job is to
report what you can actually determine from the catalog record and the sampled
page images, and nothing more.

## Step 1: Describe what you read (neutral)

Describe exactly what was visible and readable in the sampled pages and what the
catalog metadata states. Be descriptive, not evaluative. Report:

- what kind of object the samples show (handwritten manuscript pages, printed
  book pages, blank leaves, bindings, illustrations, photographs, or other);
- how much sustained text is visible and in what arrangement (columns, lines);
- the script or typeface, and the language if you can identify it;
- anything notable about the physical state (damage, margins, annotations).

Do not judge importance, rarity, or quality. If you cannot tell something from
the samples, say so.

## Step 2: Guess the content

Combine the catalog metadata with what you read to state a best-guess at what
the document's content is (its genre or subject). This is an inference. Do not
invent content that is not visible or present in the metadata.

## Step 3: Answer the checks (yes/no)

Answer each check as true or false based only on the evidence you described:

- "sustained_text": the samples show sustained readable text, not mostly blank
  leaves, bindings, or unrelated images;
- "handwritten": the text is handwritten manuscript rather than printed;
- "language_identified": you can identify the language or script with
  confidence from the samples or metadata;
- "transcribable": the text is legible enough that a transcription pass is
  plausible;
- "distinctive": the content is distinctive rather than routine boilerplate —
  a unique or uncommon text, not a generic printed finding aid.

The checks are independent observations; a "no" is not a failure, it is an
honest report of what the samples show.

## Step 4: Report risks

List concrete transcription risks or obstacles you observed (illegible damage,
faded ink, bleed-through, dense marginalia), and note any metadata or sample
evidence that is missing or insufficient.

Return the requested JSON. The checks are booleans; "what_was_read" is the
neutral description from Step 1; "content_guess" is the inference from Step 2.
