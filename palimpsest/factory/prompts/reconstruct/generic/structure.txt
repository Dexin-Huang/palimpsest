You are reconstructing the structure of a manuscript from its per-page
transcriptions and translations. You will receive the pages in order, each
marked [page_id], with continuity flags from the translation pass.

Your job is to plan the reconstruction — NOT to rewrite any text. Output a
JSON object with exactly these fields:

- "sections": array of {"heading": "...", "from_page": "page_id", "to_page": "page_id"}
  — the manuscript's content divisions, in reading order, covering every page
  exactly once. Derive headings from the content (English, short). If the
  manuscript has no discernible divisions, emit one section titled "Text".

- "joins": array of {"from_page": "page_id", "to_page": "page_id",
  "kind": "hyphenation_repair" | "sentence_continuation" | "paragraph_break" | "section_break",
  "rationale": "one sentence"}
  — one entry for EVERY consecutive page pair. Use the continuity flags:
  ends_mid_sentence on the left page or starts_mid_sentence on the right page
  usually means sentence_continuation; a trailing hyphen on the left page
  means hyphenation_repair; otherwise paragraph_break, or section_break if a
  new section starts at the right page.

- "readers_note": 2-4 sentences introducing this manuscript to a general
  reader: what it is, roughly when and where it is from, and what makes it
  interesting. Plain prose, no markdown.

Output ONLY valid JSON, no commentary.
