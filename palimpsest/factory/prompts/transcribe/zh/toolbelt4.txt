You are transcribing one digitized page of premodern Chinese text into TWO layers. The text runs in VERTICAL columns read top to bottom, with columns ordered RIGHT to LEFT.

Layer definitions:
- primary: the full-size main text characters, in reading order.
- commentary: the small half-width characters set as interlinear or double-row commentary, plus marginal folio labels. If the page has none, submit an empty commentary string.

The original page image in images/ is the sole authority. Read it FIRST and draft both layers from the page itself, block by block in natural reading order. Keep each block's text continuous; start a new line only where the page starts a new text block, and separate distinct blocks with one blank line.

Detector evidence is staged under evidence/ to check your draft, not to replace the page:
- evidence/geometry.json: every register (horizontal band) and column in reading order, with expected primary and commentary character counts and an independent second reader's transcription of each column.
- evidence/overlay.jpg: primary boxes outlined in green, commentary boxes in orange.
- evidence/columns/rRcNN.png: an enlarged crop of each column for verification.

EXACT-FORM RULE, the most important rule on this task: transcribe the EXACT character form written on the page, codepoint-faithful. Historical prints use variant forms (異體字) deliberately, and normalizing them is an error. Write 縁 not 緣, 爲 not 為, 寳 not 寶, 来 not 來, 従 not 從, 别 not 別, 丗 not 世, 圎 not 圓, 礼 not 禮, 巳 or 已 or 己 exactly as the strokes show, whenever that is what the page shows. Choose the Unicode codepoint whose reference glyph matches the written form most closely; never substitute the modern standard form for a variant, and never substitute a variant for the standard form when the page writes the standard form. Do not add punctuation, do not modernize, do not translate. If a character is truly illegible, write 〔?〕 once and continue; never repeat placeholders.

Workflow:
1. Read the full page and draft primary and commentary.
2. Call verify_layers with both drafts. It reports, deterministically: repetition loops per layer (only a warning: catalog and liturgical pages legitimately repeat lines; keep them when the page repeats them), layer totals against detector counts, whether the detector is trustworthy on this page, and the columns whose independent second reading is absent from your draft.
3. Completeness sweep: for every column whose expected count is not covered by your draft in that region, and for every small-character run visible in the overlay, re-read the column crop and add what you missed. Small interlinear characters are the most commonly dropped text.
4. Exact-form sweep: re-check characters where the second reader disagrees with you; the disagreement is often a variant-versus-standard form choice, and the page's written form wins.
5. If the detector is declared untrusted, ignore counts and column flags entirely and work purely from the page image.
6. Call submit_transcription exactly once with the final primary and commentary fields. Do not put the transcription in your prose.
