## Why This Matters

This is not generic catalog filler. The surviving leaves preserve a small but vivid ritual world:

- pediatric illness understood through spirit attack
- named female or demon-like beings linked to dream omens
- symptom-pattern diagnosis
- ritual sacrifice / appeasement
- talismanic protection
- mixed Chinese plus Indic-script material
- painted zoomorphic guardian / afflicting figures

This is exactly the kind of "vanished world" witness Palimpsest is meant to recover: not only doctrine, but practical human fear, healing, childhood vulnerability, and ritual response.

## Stable Findings

- This is a real ritual-healing witness, not just a museum label effect.
- The leaves are formulaic enough to support comparative reconstruction.
- The object is multilingual in practice:
  - Chinese explanation / diagnosis
  - Indic-script material likely containing names, mantras, or dharanis
- The pictures are integral to the diagnostic logic, not mere decoration.
- The central punctures are structurally important and support the idea of a bound or coordinated set.
