## Recipes (f008r)

### Papaya Jam (做木瓜法)

Original: Peel papaya, boil until soft, mash. For every 1 jin (~600g) papaya, use 2 jin sugar. Simmer into paste.

**Modern version:**
- 500g ripe papaya, peeled and seeded
- 1kg white sugar
- Boil papaya until soft, mash, add sugar, simmer 45-60 min until thick

Very sweet. Spreads thin.

### Walnut-Olive Cakes (做欖仁餅法)

Original uses Chinese olive kernels (hard to find). Substitute almonds.

**Modern version:**
- 150g flour
- 150g walnuts + 150g almonds, ground
- 3 eggs, 300g sugar
- Mix, press into molds, bake 170°C for 20-25 min

Dense, rich tea cakes.

## Medal Grading System (f008r)

For the Abbé de Rothelin, a French coin collector:

- O = poorly preserved
- Ø = very poorly preserved
- OO = neither good nor bad
- OOO = well preserved
- OOOO = mint condition ("fleur de coin")

## Potentially New / Understudied (f008v)

**Montesquieu received a copy of the Chinese Chronology directly from Beijing.**

The distribution list on f008v shows "Monsieur le Président de Montesquieu" receiving 1 copy. This is a direct, documented transmission from Jesuit missionaries to the philosopher. Worth cross-referencing with Montesquieu scholarship.

**Chevalier Decham** received 12 copies to redistribute — a key node in the network. Who was he?

**The King of England** also received a copy ("au Roy d'Angleterre"). Which king? George I or II? When?

Other recipients worth tracing: Cardinal Passionei (famous bibliophile), Abbé Bignon (head of French Royal Library), Cardinal Fleury (chief minister of France).

**Detailed Account of Papal Audience, October 2, 1719 (f033v)**

A point-by-point account of Pope Clement XI's audience with Jesuit leadership regarding the Chinese Rites Controversy. Attendees: Father General, five assistants, Father Tamburin, Procurator of Propaganda Fide, Secretary Caraffa.

Extraordinary details include:
- "Un long silence qui dura une bonne heure et demie" — an hour and a half of silence in the room
- Pope's demand for "parfaite obéissance" from China missionaries
- Pope's acknowledgment that Cardinal Tournon acted "avec trop de rigueur"
- Discussion of the Bishop of Beijing's compromise on ancestral rites
- Reference to Pope's 1717 letter to the King of France

Source chain: Father Contancin shared the account with the notebook's author on January 15, 1720 — just months after the audience.

This level of granular, eyewitness-style detail may be unpublished. Worth cross-referencing with Rites Controversy scholarship.
